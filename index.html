Common questions

Is this a real, clinical IQ test?

No. BrainIQ Pro gives an estimate for personal insight and fun. It is not clinically validated. For an official assessment, see a licensed psychologist.

How is my IQ score calculated?

Your share of correct answers is mapped onto the standard IQ scale (average 100). Online estimates can be off by several points, so treat the number as a rough guide.

How long does a test take?

From 10 minutes for the short models up to 60 minutes for Mensa Prep. One timer runs across all questions, and unanswered ones count as skipped when time ends.

Do I need an account?

Brain games are open to everyone. For IQ tests you sign in with Google or email, so your score, history and certificate are saved.

Can I retake a model?

Yes, as often as you like. A model keeps the same questions, so try a different model if you want fresh ones.

Which skills do the games train?

Working memory, reaction speed, focus, hand-eye coordination and visual perception. Games are practice, not a replacement for a test.
